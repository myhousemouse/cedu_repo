# Project brief

Status: Draft

## Project objective

Confirmed: Analyze VOCA TEST, a learning-content authoring tool teachers use to
create student vocabulary tests from words in CEDU books. Identify
evidence-based opportunities to improve that authoring experience.

The proposals must be traceable to observed evidence, user needs, market
context, or clearly labeled hypotheses.

## Target users

Confirmed primary user: a teacher who selects words from CEDU books and
configures a test for students.

Secondary stakeholder: the student who takes the exported or printed test, but
the student-facing learning experience is not the primary analysis target.

## Desired outcome

Produce a defensible assessment of the current VOCA TEST authoring experience
and a prioritized set of improvements that reduce teacher effort and errors
while increasing confidence in the generated test.

## Value proposition

Confirmed baseline value: teachers can reuse trusted vocabulary from CEDU
books to produce student tests instead of rebuilding the content manually.

Open question: which part of test authoring creates the most teacher effort or
error—book setup, range selection, item/type configuration, preview/editing,
printing/download, or reuse?

## Scope

### In scope

- VOCA TEST's teacher-facing test-authoring workflow
- Its entry and account relationship with `https://www.cedubook.com/`
- Book registration/selection, unit and word range, question-type and item-count
  configuration, preview/editing, save, print/download, and prior-test reuse
- Teacher goals, authoring efficiency, error prevention, and output confidence
- Identifying strengths, friction, gaps, and unmet needs
- Reviewing relevant competitors and market signals
- Proposing and prioritizing existing-service improvements
- Proposing new service concepts supported by the findings

### Out of scope

- Application implementation during the planning and research phase
- VOCA TEST Play / 보카플's student learning, QR, game, result, and review flows
  except where they clarify the distinction between services
- Broad redesign of CEDU Group, CEDU Learn, or the whole portfolio
- Presenting unsupported assumptions as user or market facts
- Finalizing a solution before the current-state analysis is complete

## Success measures

- Every major finding identifies its source and confidence level.
- Recommendations trace back to findings or explicitly labeled hypotheses.
- Improvement ideas and new-service concepts are prioritized using consistent
  criteria.
- Unknowns and research limitations remain visible.

## Constraints

- Authenticated access is required to verify the current authoring interface.
- Relevant comparison scope should prioritize teacher content-authoring and
  test-generation tools, not general student learning apps.
- Access to non-public analytics, customer feedback, or internal strategy may
  be unavailable.
- Historical vault material may describe an earlier service state and must be
  date-checked against current official sources.
