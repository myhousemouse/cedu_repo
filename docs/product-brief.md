# Project brief

Status: Draft

## Project objective

Confirmed: Investigate and analyze CEDU's existing services, identify
opportunities for improvement, and propose credible new-service concepts.

The proposals must be traceable to observed evidence, user needs, market
context, or clearly labeled hypotheses.

## Target users

Baseline finding: the portfolio serves at least students, teachers/academy
operators, parents or other purchasers, and institutional administrators. The
learner, buyer, and operator are not always the same person.

Open question: Which actor should the next phase prioritize?

## Desired outcome

Produce a defensible assessment of the current service portfolio and a
prioritized set of improvement and new-service opportunities.

## Value proposition

Baseline interpretation: CEDU's distinctive asset is the continuity from a
broad, structured English-book catalogue into digital practice, assessment,
review, and teacher operations. Research must still establish where users
experience that portfolio as continuous versus fragmented.

## Scope

### In scope

- Inventorying CEDU's existing services and customer touchpoints
- Reviewing `https://www.cedubook.com/`, `https://cedugroup.com/`, and
  `https://cedulearn.com/` as distinct web properties
- Analyzing target users, value propositions, user journeys, content, and UX
- Identifying strengths, friction, gaps, and unmet needs
- Reviewing relevant competitors and market signals
- Proposing and prioritizing existing-service improvements
- Proposing new service concepts supported by the findings

### Out of scope

- Application implementation during the planning and research phase
- Presenting unsupported assumptions as user or market facts
- Finalizing a solution before the current-state analysis is complete

## Success measures

- Every major finding identifies its source and confidence level.
- Recommendations trace back to findings or explicitly labeled hypotheses.
- Improvement ideas and new-service concepts are prioritized using consistent
  criteria.
- Unknowns and research limitations remain visible.

## Constraints

- Scope beyond the three supplied official websites is not yet confirmed.
- Access to non-public analytics, customer feedback, or internal strategy may
  be unavailable.
- Historical vault material may describe an earlier service state and must be
  date-checked against current official sources.
