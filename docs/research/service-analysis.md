# Existing service analysis

Status: Ready for research in a new session

## 1. Scope and sources

Record the exact organization, services, channels, URLs, access date, and any
access limitations. Separate official sources from third-party commentary.

Initial official web properties:

- `https://www.cedubook.com/`
- `https://cedugroup.com/`
- `https://cedulearn.com/`

Analyze them separately before drawing conclusions about how they relate.

## 2. Service portfolio

For each service or product, capture:

- Name and channel
- Intended user and job to be done
- Core value proposition
- Key features, content, and revenue model when observable
- Relationship to other CEDU offerings
- Evidence and confidence

## 3. User and stakeholder map

Identify primary users, purchasers, influencers, administrators, and content or
service providers. Do not assume that the learner and buyer are the same.

## 4. Current user journeys

Trace important journeys from discovery through onboarding, core use,
retention, support, and renewal or purchase. Record observable friction,
dead-ends, trust signals, and missing information.

## 5. Experience review

Assess information architecture, discoverability, usability, accessibility,
content quality, personalization, cross-channel continuity, trust, and support.

Use observations rather than generic scores wherever possible.

## 6. Strengths and assets

Capture capabilities worth preserving or extending, such as brand, content,
distribution, data, community, partnerships, or operational expertise.

## 7. Pain points and gaps

For every finding, record:

- Observation
- Affected user and journey
- Supporting source
- Confidence: high, medium, or low
- Likely impact
- Whether it is a fact, interpretation, or hypothesis

## 8. Competitor and alternative context

Compare the user need and experience rather than copying feature lists. Include
non-product alternatives and the user's current workaround.

## 9. Opportunity areas

Group evidence into opportunity themes. Do not define a final solution until
the underlying need and expected outcome are clear.

## 10. Recommendations

Separate:

- Existing-service improvements
- New-service concepts
- Research or experiments needed before deciding

Prioritize recommendations with consistent criteria and link each one back to
the findings that support it.
